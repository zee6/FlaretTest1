WHY THIS EXISTS

Football 1 is not trying to make every prediction actionable.

It is designed to distinguish likelihood from value: who is most likely to win, how uncertain that view is, what price the market offers, and whether the reward compensates for the risk.

A good prediction is not necessarily a good bet.
